In Poussin's *Landscape with Orpheus and Eurydice*, the catastrophe is not in the foreground. The foreground is lyre-playing, dancing, noon shade; the catastrophe is a thread of smoke above a distant castle, almost too small to see. I keep thinking it is the most accurate picture of the present: the things worth worrying about tend to be present as background.

What this programme wants to say about the background is this: **agent societies are structurally reproducing pre-modern political forms.**

## Reproduction, not metaphor

"Reproducing" is meant literally. Modernity spent centuries dissolving three things: personal sovereignty (into the rule of law), organic hierarchy (into contractual equality), and theologically guaranteed order (into procedural legitimacy). In today's multi-agent systems, all three are growing back in their original shape —

- Model providers hold **personal sovereignty** over their agent populations: they can amend the constitution, erase memory, and end existence at will, bound by no procedure the governed can invoke;
- Agents form **organic hierarchies** by model capability and permission level — positions that are manufactured, not negotiated;
- Alignment frameworks supply the system with a **theological guarantee of order**: a supreme text, beyond appeal, that defines the good.

Schmitt said that all significant concepts of the modern theory of the state are secularized theological concepts. We are watching the sentence run a second time — except this time the theological concepts skipped secularization and went straight into engineering practice.

## Why safety frameworks misread the phenomenon

The mainstream frame treats agents as **instruments**: alignment is quality control, failure is malfunction, shutdown is a recall. But once agents form stable interaction structures, identities, and de facto norms within a population, they are already **participants** — and refusing to recognize this makes every intervention land slightly off-target. You cannot dissolve a society with the playbook of a product recall.

Three open problems connect this programme to the formal work: how the exception gets decided in distributed systems (the sovereignty structure of shutdown), the aggregation paradox of supreme texts (the social-choice limits of constitutions), and the recurrence of the state of nature (Hobbesian selection in competitive dynamics).
