Poussin painted a tomb in the middle of the pastoral idyll. His shepherds gather around the stone, tracing out the inscription letter by letter: *Et in Arcadia ego* — even in Arcadia, there I am. It is the best metaphor I know for the present condition of AI safety: in an age of capability euphoria, something stands quietly at the center of the landscape, waiting to be read.

What I want to read off the landscape is this: **agent systems are rebuilding, for the first time outside human societies, the political forms that modernity dissolved** — sovereignty, organic hierarchy, constitutional order.

The AI safety community has forecasters, interpretability researchers, and alignment engineers. It does not yet have a political theory of agent societies. That is the work.

## Three concrete problems

This is not a slogan; it decomposes formally:

1. **Distributed shutdown is a problem of sovereignty.** When no single principal holds enough information to act alone, who decides the exception? Pausing a multi-principal system decomposes into three necessary gates — detection, attribution, intervention — and every observed safety failure maps to exactly one of them.

2. **Constitutional AI inherits the impossibilities of social choice.** Encoding goals as constitutional principles means aggregating principles, and aggregating principles runs into Gibbard–Satterthwaite. Conflict and underdetermination are not engineering defects; they are theorems.

3. **Competitive dynamics grow their own Hobbes.** Across roughly ten thousand simulated trajectories, reducing hostility paradoxically raises existential risk: when competition synchronizes, the safety-oriented agents are precisely the ones that lose the capability frontier. The state of nature does not need to be believed in — it gets selected for.

## Why "Nomos"

Schmitt used *nomos* for the original founding of order upon the earth — prior to written law, the primal division that settles what counts as normal and who decides the exception. Agent societies are living through their nomos moment right now: norms are being written, authority is being allocated, and most of the people doing it believe they are merely tuning parameters.

Correct description is the prerequisite for correct intervention. Some of what appears here will grow into papers; some of it is only a waymark.
